# R-12 Billing, payments and accounting basics

**Evidence A: established** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 4 · Get paid

## Why it matters
Revenue counts only when it is collected, recorded and reported correctly.

## How it works
Invoice clearly, use a regulated payment processor, keep records of every transaction, reconcile accounts, and set money aside for taxes. In the EU and in Croatia, income from freelance or business work is generally taxable, VAT may apply (including on sales to customers in other EU countries), and Croatia has its own invoicing and fiscalisation rules and several ways to operate (for example as a sole trader or a one-person company) with different tax and contribution rules. Verify current details with the Croatian Tax Administration and a Croatian accountant.

## What you can change
Processor, invoicing cadence, bookkeeping tool.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Set up a practice ledger, issue sample invoices, and reconcile them.

## Proof it works
Records match bank statements to the cent and invoices follow the applicable national rules.

**Guardrail:** never hold or move money without human authorisation.
