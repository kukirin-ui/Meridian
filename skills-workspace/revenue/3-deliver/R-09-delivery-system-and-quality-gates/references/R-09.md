# R-09 Delivery system and quality gates

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 3 · Deliver

## Why it matters
Reputation and repeat income depend on checked, on-time delivery.

## How it works
Use the skills of Agents 1 to 3 inside a fixed pipeline: written acceptance criteria, automated checks for code, proofreading and source verification for text, visual review for graphics, then a human check before anything is sent.

## What you can change
Gate list, review depth, turnaround target.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Run three pilot deliveries and log every defect found at each gate.

## Proof it works
Defects found by the customer fall to near zero across pilots.

**Guardrail:** nothing leaves without the final gate.
