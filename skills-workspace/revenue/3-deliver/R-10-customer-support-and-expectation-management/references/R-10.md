# R-10 Customer support and expectation management

**Evidence C: emerging** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 3 · Deliver

## Why it matters
Clear communication after the sale prevents refunds and disputes.

## How it works
Confirm scope at the start, give regular status updates, answer quickly, handle problems openly, and have written policies for revisions, refunds and escalation to a human.

## What you can change
Response time, revision allowance, refund policy.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Role-play support cases and review tone and accuracy.

## Proof it works
Customers can state what to expect and when.

**Guardrail:** escalate disputes and legal threats to a human.
