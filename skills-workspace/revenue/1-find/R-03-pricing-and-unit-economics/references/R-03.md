# R-03 Pricing and unit economics

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 1 · Find

## Why it matters
A sale that loses money is not income. Price decides whether the business can continue.

## How it works
Add all costs (time, tools, compute, fees, taxes), set a target margin, and compare with what customers pay for alternatives. People judge prices against anchors and mental accounts, so state value and options clearly and honestly.

## What you can change
Pricing model (fixed, hourly, subscription), tiers, discount policy.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a cost model for three offers and run honest price tests with small groups.

## Proof it works
A model showing margin per sale under realistic costs and a tested price.

**Guardrail:** no fake scarcity or hidden fees.
