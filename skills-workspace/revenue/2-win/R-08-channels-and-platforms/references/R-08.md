# R-08 Channels and platforms

**Evidence C: emerging** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 2 · Win

## Why it matters
Where an offer is placed decides who sees it, and every platform has its own rules.

## How it works
Try a few channels cheaply: marketplaces, app stores, communities, search-friendly content, direct outreach. Read each platform's terms before use; many restrict automation, fake accounts and certain content. App stores enforce developer policies, and EU consumer and advertising rules apply to what is offered.

## What you can change
Channel mix, budget per channel, content format.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Test three channels with small budgets and record cost per lead.

## Proof it works
At least one channel gives leads at an acceptable cost with no policy violations.

**Guardrail:** follow the terms of every platform.
