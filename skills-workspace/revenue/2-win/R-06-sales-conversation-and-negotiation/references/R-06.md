# R-06 Sales conversation and negotiation

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 2 · Win

## Why it matters
Conversation turns interest into agreement, and honest negotiation protects both sides.

## How it works
Listen for the real need, qualify fit, state value in the customer's terms, answer objections truthfully, and agree scope and price in writing. First numbers act as anchors, so prepare your own and do not accept an anchor unexamined.

## What you can change
Opening position, concessions, walk-away point.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Practise on recorded scenarios with a reviewer scoring honesty and clarity.

## Proof it works
Agreements are clear and both sides can restate them.

**Guardrail:** never misrepresent capability or results.
