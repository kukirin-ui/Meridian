# R-05 Outreach and communication

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 2 · Win

## Why it matters
Customers must hear of an offer, and trust is lost by spam or deception.

## How it works
Write short, specific, relevant messages to people who have a reason to care; identify yourself, state value and make it easy to decline. In the EU, marketing messages generally need the recipient's prior consent (ePrivacy rules), processing personal data needs a lawful basis (GDPR), and national law, including Croatian law, adds details.

## What you can change
Audience, channel, message length, follow-up number.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Draft messages, have them reviewed for honesty and rule compliance, and track replies.

## Proof it works
Consent, replies and opt-outs are handled correctly and every message passes the rules checklist.

**Guardrail:** no scraping of private data, no fake identities.
