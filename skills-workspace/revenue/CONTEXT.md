# Revenue Layer — routing (`revenue`)

**Revenue Layer (shared by all agents)** · Shared pipeline · Engine: honest value, checked delivery

The Revenue Layer gives all three agents the business skills to turn checked work into lawful income: find real demand, make an honest offer, win customers, deliver with quality gates, get paid and stay compliant, then measure and protect. It does not guarantee income: most online ventures earn little or nothing, platforms restrict some automation, and legal and tax rules depend on the country (EU and Croatian sources are used as examples).

Every stage obeys R-16: no payment, contract, new account, public post or message to a new person without human approval, with spending caps, audit logs and a named accountable human.

**Shape.** Revenue pipeline: the same five stages serve all three agents. Guardrails on every stage: R-15 and R-16.

**Where to start.** R-16 before any other skill is used with real money.
