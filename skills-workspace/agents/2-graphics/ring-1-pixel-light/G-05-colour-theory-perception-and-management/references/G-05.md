# G-05 Colour theory, perception and management

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 1 · Pixel and light

## Why it matters
Colour sets mood, hierarchy and accessibility, and it changes between screens and print.

## How it works
Hue, value and saturation; perceptual colour spaces for even gradients; contrast for legibility; colour-blind safety; colour profiles and conversion between screen and print.

## Ways to vary it
Colour space, palette size, contrast target, profile.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Rebuild ten palettes in a perceptual space, check contrast, and soft-proof for print.

## Signs of mastery
Palettes read clearly, pass contrast checks, and survive conversion.

**Failure mode:** colours that look fine on one screen only.
