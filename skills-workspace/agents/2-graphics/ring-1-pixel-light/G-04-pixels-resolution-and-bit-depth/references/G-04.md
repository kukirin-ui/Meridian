# G-04 Pixels, resolution and bit depth

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 1 · Pixel and light

## Why it matters
Everything on screen is a grid of samples. Understanding the grid prevents blur, banding and wrong sizes.

## How it works
Pixels per inch versus pixel dimensions, scaling and resampling, anti-aliasing, alpha transparency, bit depth and banding, and dithering. Choose the working size for the final use before starting.

## Ways to vary it
Working resolution, resampling method, bit depth, transparency handling.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Export the same artwork for phone, print and web and inspect the differences at pixel level.

## Signs of mastery
Outputs stay sharp at the target size with no banding or fringes.

**Failure mode:** scaling raster images up and calling it high resolution.
