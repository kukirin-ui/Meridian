# G-10 Drawing and mark-making

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 2 · Mark and type

## Why it matters
Drawing is thinking on paper. It trains observation of shape, proportion and perspective.

## How it works
Construct forms from simple solids, use perspective and proportion, make confident lines, and draw from reference and imagination. Practice is repetitive and graded.

## Ways to vary it
Medium, speed, reference versus invention.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Follow a structured free course and keep dated drawings; compare early and late work.

## Signs of mastery
Drawings keep consistent perspective and proportion.

**Failure mode:** stiff outlines with no construction underneath.
