# G-12 Icon and symbol design

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 2 · Mark and type

## Why it matters
Small symbols carry meaning instantly across languages.

## How it works
Grid and keylines, consistent stroke and corner rules, simple metaphors, optical alignment, and checks at small sizes and in dark and light modes.

## Ways to vary it
Grid size, stroke weight, fill style, metaphor choice.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Design a set of forty icons, then test recognition with readers.

## Signs of mastery
Icons are recognised at 16 pixels and look like one family.

**Failure mode:** clever but ambiguous metaphors.
