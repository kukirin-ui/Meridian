# G-01 The ideation engine

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Imagination core · ideas, taste, direction

## Why it matters
Every visual task begins with finding an idea worth making, and the first idea is rarely the best one.

## How it works
Gather references and a moodboard, diverge widely (many sketches, metaphors, constraints, unlikely combinations), cluster, then pick a few directions to develop. Sampling variety and forced association widen the set; a structured brief keeps it relevant.

## Ways to vary it
Number of ideas, constraint type, reference distance, which directions are carried forward.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Run daily thumbnail and concept rounds from set briefs; score fluency, flexibility and originality; compare with single-pass attempts.

## Signs of mastery
Idea sets beat a single pass on blind originality while fitting the brief.

**Failure mode:** variety with no fit to purpose.
