# G-35 Style, reference, originality and rights

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 6 · Machine imagination and the unknown

## Why it matters
Creative work must be original enough to be yours, and legal to use and sell.

## How it works
Study styles by their principles, not by tracing; keep records of references and licences; check outputs for near copies; understand that EU copyright protects a work that is its author's own intellectual creation, so purely machine-generated output may not qualify, that EU rules govern use of training data, that the AI Act adds transparency duties, and that image models can reproduce training images in rare cases. Rules vary by country and change.

## Ways to vary it
Reference policy, licence set, similarity threshold, disclosure.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Log references and licences for every project; run similarity checks on outputs.

## Signs of mastery
Every asset has a licence record and no near-copy passes review.

**Failure mode:** unlicensed or copied imagery.
