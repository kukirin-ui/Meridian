# G-36 Entering an unknown visual category

**Evidence C: emerging** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 6 · Machine imagination and the unknown

## Why it matters
New media, tools and formats appear constantly. The master skill is to become competent in any of them fast.

## How it works
Identify what the medium is made of (units, constraints, tools, standards), find the three best references and the official documentation, copy the fundamentals by deliberate exercises, make a small original piece, get critique, then repeat at larger scale. Map it to known skills by analogy and note where analogies break.

## Ways to vary it
How far the new medium is from known ones, time per cycle, critique source.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6 M7. Enter one unfamiliar medium every month with a fixed protocol and compare time to competence.

## Signs of mastery
A critiqued, original piece in a new medium within days, with the method documented.

**Failure mode:** shallow imitation of surface style.
