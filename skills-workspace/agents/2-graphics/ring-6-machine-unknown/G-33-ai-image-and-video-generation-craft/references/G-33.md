# G-33 AI image and video generation craft

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 6 · Machine imagination and the unknown

## Why it matters
Generative models are fast creative tools whose output must be directed, edited and judged by people.

## How it works
Diffusion models start from noise and refine it under text or image guidance. Control comes from prompts, guidance scale, reference images, structural control inputs, small fine-tunes of a subject or style, and inpainting. Evaluate with human review.

## Ways to vary it
Model, guidance strength, control inputs, seed, resolution, fine-tune.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Reproduce briefs with several models and controls, keep seed and settings logs, and compare with human-made versions.

## Signs of mastery
Controlled, repeatable results that match the brief.

**Failure mode:** uncontrolled 'slot machine' output and anatomical or text errors.
