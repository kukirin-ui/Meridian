# G-18 Diagrams, infographics and data visualisation

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 3 · Image and composition

## Why it matters
Visual explanations compress complexity if they encode data accurately.

## How it works
Choose the question, pick encodings that people read accurately (position over area over colour), use colour scales safe for colour-blind readers, annotate the message, and avoid distortion.

## Ways to vary it
Chart type, scale, annotation, interaction.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Redraw poor graphics and test comprehension with readers.

## Signs of mastery
Readers answer questions faster and more accurately.

**Failure mode:** decoration that hides the data.
