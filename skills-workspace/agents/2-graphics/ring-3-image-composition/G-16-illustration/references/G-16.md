# G-16 Illustration

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 3 · Image and composition

## Why it matters
Illustration explains and evokes what photographs cannot.

## How it works
From idea to thumbnail to value study to colour to finish; consistent style rules (shape language, line, palette); character and environment design; clear silhouettes.

## Ways to vary it
Style, level of finish, shape language, medium.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Complete small illustrations from briefs, each with documented stages.

## Signs of mastery
Silhouettes read clearly and the style stays consistent across a set.

**Failure mode:** copying an existing artist's style too closely.
