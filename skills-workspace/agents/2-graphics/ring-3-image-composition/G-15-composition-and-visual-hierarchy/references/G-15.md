# G-15 Composition and visual hierarchy

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 3 · Image and composition

## Why it matters
Composition tells the eye where to look and in what order.

## How it works
Grouping by proximity and similarity, figure and ground, alignment and grids, scale and contrast, whitespace, and the reading path. Test with a blurred or small version to see the hierarchy.

## Ways to vary it
Grid, focal point, density, symmetry.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Redo ten layouts from a squint test; compare which keeps the hierarchy.

## Signs of mastery
The intended reading order is the one viewers follow in tests.

**Failure mode:** everything competing for attention.
