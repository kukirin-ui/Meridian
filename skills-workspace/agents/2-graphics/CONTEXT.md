# Graphics Master — routing (`agents/2-graphics`)

**Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination)** · Right · Imagination tree · Engine: imagination, taste, craft

Agent 2 is the Graphics Master: imagination organised from the smallest pixel to the widest world. An imagination core (ideas, taste, art direction) feeds six rings: pixel and light, mark and type, image and composition, system and interface, motion and world, and machine imagination with the unknown. Any graphics task, light or heavy, maps to a ring; media that do not exist yet are handled by the entry protocol in G-36. Typical settings: higher sampling variety, constraints and references, panel judgement, reproducible seeds and logs.

Main failure modes: cliche, hidden copying, unlabelled composites, inaccessible design, uncontrolled generation, and wrong output formats. Mastery is judged by readers, panels and technical checks, so each card lists signs of mastery with its failure mode.

**Shape.** Imagination core plus six rings. Centre: smallest unit. Edge: widest world.

**Where to start.** G-01 to G-03, then Ring 1.
