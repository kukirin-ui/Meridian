# G-24 Presentation and information design

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 4 · System and interface

## Why it matters
Slides, posters and reports are how most people receive ideas.

## How it works
One message per slide, strong hierarchy, minimal text, consistent templates, data-ink discipline, speaker support, and accessible reading order.

## Ways to vary it
Density, template flexibility, narrative structure.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Rebuild text-heavy decks as clear visual stories and test comprehension.

## Signs of mastery
Audience recall of the key message improves.

**Failure mode:** slides as documents.
