# G-25 Marketing, social and advertising graphics

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 4 · System and interface

## Why it matters
Visual output must fit each platform's format and the law, and it must earn attention honestly.

## How it works
Know the sizes, safe zones and file limits of each channel, design for a quick glance, test variants, disclose sponsorship as EU rules on unfair commercial practices require, and avoid misleading design.

## Ways to vary it
Format set, variant count, call to action, disclosure.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a set of variants for each channel and compare engagement honestly.

## Signs of mastery
Assets meet platform specifications and legal disclosure rules.

**Failure mode:** dark patterns and deceptive claims.
