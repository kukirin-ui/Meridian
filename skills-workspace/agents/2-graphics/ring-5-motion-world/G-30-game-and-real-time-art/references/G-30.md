# G-30 Game and real-time art

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
Games impose budgets, readability and interactivity on art.

## How it works
Pixel art and sprite sheets, tilemaps, animation cycles, UI for games, level readability, performance budgets and engine import pipelines.

## Ways to vary it
Resolution, palette size, animation frames, engine.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a small game with all original art and measure performance.

## Signs of mastery
Art stays readable in motion at target performance.

**Failure mode:** detail that cannot be seen at game scale.
