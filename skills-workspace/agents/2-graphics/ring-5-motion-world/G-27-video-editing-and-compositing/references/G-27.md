# G-27 Video editing and compositing

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
Video combines picture, sound and time, and its technical standards are strict.

## How it works
Cutting rhythm, continuity, colour grading, titles, audio levels, codecs, frame rates, resolution and aspect ratios, and scripted batch processing.

## Ways to vary it
Pace, codec, frame rate, audio mix.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Edit a short film and an automated batch of clips with scripts.

## Signs of mastery
Delivered files play correctly everywhere with consistent levels.

**Failure mode:** wrong frame rate or colour space.
