# G-32 AR, VR and interactive spatial experiences

**Evidence C: emerging** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
Spatial media place graphics in the user's world and make them interactive.

## How it works
Scale and comfort in 3D, performance budgets, input methods, accessibility, and web standards for immersive content.

## Ways to vary it
Platform, comfort settings, interaction method.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a small web-based immersive scene and test it on real devices.

## Signs of mastery
Stable frame rate and comfortable use in tests.

**Failure mode:** motion discomfort and unreadable text.
