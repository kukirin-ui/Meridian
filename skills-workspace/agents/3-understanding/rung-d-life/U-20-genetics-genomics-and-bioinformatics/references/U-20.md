# U-20 Genetics, genomics and bioinformatics

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
Heredity and sequence data link molecules to traits, disease and evolution.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** inheritance, variation, sequencing, alignment, statistics for genomes, and genome editing.

**Intuition half:** the genome as a text with meaning in context. Privacy and ethics rules apply to human data.

## What you can change
Organism, data source, statistical threshold.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Analyse a public genome region with standard tools and compare with the database annotation.

## Bridge test
Matches database annotations and states uncertainty and ethical limits.
