# U-21 Evolution and ecology

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
Evolution organises all of biology, and ecology explains how living things interact.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** selection, drift, population genetics, phylogenetic trees and ecosystem models.

**Intuition half:** branching histories and webs of interaction. Fit models to data and compare alternatives.

## What you can change
Model class, time scale, assumptions about selection.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a tiny evolutionary simulation and compare it with real population data.

## Bridge test
Simulation behaviour matches theory and real data patterns are explained by named mechanisms.
