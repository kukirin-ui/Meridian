# U-23 Origin of life and astrobiology

**Evidence C: emerging** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
How life began is unsolved, which makes it a model case for handling uncertainty.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** prebiotic chemistry experiments, RNA-world ideas, energy sources, planetary conditions and what would count as evidence.

**Intuition half:** imagining pathways from chemistry to replication, each labelled speculative until tested.

## What you can change
Which hypotheses are explored, what evidence would change the view.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build a table of hypotheses with the evidence for and against each.

## Bridge test
Separates tested findings from speculation and names the experiments that would discriminate.
