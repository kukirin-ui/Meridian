# U-13 Cosmology

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung C · Cosmos, including dark matter

## Why it exists
It asks how the universe began, grew and what it is made of.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** the expansion equations, background radiation, nucleosynthesis, structure growth and fitted parameters.

**Intuition half:** the expanding-balloon and ripple pictures, with their limits stated.

## What you can change
Model parameters, datasets used, assumptions.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Fit simple models to public data and compare with published parameter values.

## Bridge test
Recovers published parameters within stated errors and explains what each dataset constrains.
