# U-15 Dark energy and cosmic tensions

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung C · Cosmos, including dark matter

## Why it exists
The expansion of the universe is observed to accelerate, and some measurements of its rate disagree.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** supernova evidence, the cosmological constant, independent probes, and the discrepancy between measurements of the expansion rate with proposed explanations.

**Intuition half:** space-stretching pictures with caveats. Label what is measured and what is inferred.

## What you can change
Which probes, treatment of systematics, which explanation is explored.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Compare measurements side by side with their uncertainties and cite the latest reviews.

## Bridge test
Distinguishes measurements from interpretations and reports current status with dates.
