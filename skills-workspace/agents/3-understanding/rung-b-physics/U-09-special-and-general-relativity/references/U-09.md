# U-09 Special and general relativity

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
Relativity redefines space and time, and its corrections are needed in practical systems such as satellite navigation.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** Lorentz transformations, spacetime intervals, the metric, geodesics and curvature.

**Intuition half:** spacetime diagrams, light cones and the picture of mass bending the paths of free objects. Gravitational waves are a direct test.

## What you can change
Level of tensor formalism, weak-field approximations.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Work through lecture notes and compute time dilation and orbital effects numerically.

## Bridge test
Correctly computes relativistic effects and explains each with a spacetime diagram.
