# U-07 Mechanics, thermodynamics and statistical physics

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
These theories explain motion, heat and why macroscopic behaviour emerges from countless microscopic parts.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, conservation laws, entropy, the Boltzmann distribution.

**Intuition half:** energy landscapes, flows and the picture of probability spreading into more possibilities.

## What you can change
Formalism, which approximations, equilibrium or not.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Solve problems, then simulate a gas or a pendulum and compare with the equations.

## Bridge test
Analytical and simulated answers agree and the entropy argument is explained in words.
