# U-12 Quantum field theory and the Standard Model

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
The Standard Model describes known particles and three of the four forces, and it also shows where physics is incomplete.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** fields, symmetries and gauge principles, particle content, interactions and measured parameters.

**Intuition half:** particles as excitations of fields and forces as exchanges. Known gaps include gravity, dark matter and the origin of matter-antimatter imbalance.
