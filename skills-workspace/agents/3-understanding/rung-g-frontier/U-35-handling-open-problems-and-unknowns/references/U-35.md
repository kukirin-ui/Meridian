# U-35 Handling open problems and unknowns

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung G · Frontier and control

## Why it exists
Frontier topics invite hype, false certainty and pseudoscience.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** label every statement as established, supported, speculative or unknown; ask what evidence would change the view; re-check current literature before stating frontier claims.

**Intuition half:** staying curious about alternatives without endorsing them.

## What you can change
Evidence thresholds, how often claims are refreshed.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Write status reports on five open problems with dated sources.

## Bridge test
No open problem is presented as solved and each status report is dated and sourced.
