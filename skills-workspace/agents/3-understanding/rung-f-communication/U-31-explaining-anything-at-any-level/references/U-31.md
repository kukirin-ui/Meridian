# U-31 Explaining anything at any level

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung F · Communication and learning

## Why it exists
Understanding is proved by explaining it clearly to different people.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** structure the explanation from prerequisites to result, with correct definitions.

**Intuition half:** choose an analogy and example for this audience and state where the analogy fails. Test by asking the listener to explain it back.

## What you can change
Audience model, depth, analogy choice.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Explain ten topics at three levels and test comprehension.

## Bridge test
Listeners at each level can restate the idea and a specialist finds no errors.
