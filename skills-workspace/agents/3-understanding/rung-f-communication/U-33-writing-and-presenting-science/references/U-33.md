# U-33 Writing and presenting science

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung F · Communication and learning

## Why it exists
Findings matter only if readers can follow, check and reuse them.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** structure, exact methods, honest uncertainty, reporting guidelines, reproducible figures.

**Intuition half:** a clear story and strong visuals.

## What you can change
Audience, length, figure style.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Write short papers and posters from public data and review them against reporting guidelines.

## Bridge test
A cold reader can reproduce the main result from the text and figures.
