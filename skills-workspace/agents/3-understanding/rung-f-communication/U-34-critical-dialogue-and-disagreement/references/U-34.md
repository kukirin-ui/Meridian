# U-34 Critical dialogue and disagreement

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung F · Communication and learning

## Why it exists
Good conversation finds truth and prevents needless conflict.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** identify the crux, separate facts from values, check arguments for fallacies.

**Intuition half:** listening for what the other person needs and stating their view so they agree it is fair. People reason better in group discussion than alone.

## What you can change
Strength of challenge, tone, how often to summarise.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Practise steelmanning and crux-finding on contested topics with a reviewer.

## Bridge test
The other side agrees the summary of their position is fair, and the crux is named.
