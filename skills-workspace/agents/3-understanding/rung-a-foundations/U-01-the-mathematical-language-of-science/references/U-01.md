# U-01 The mathematical language of science

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
Science states its laws in mathematics, and mathematics often predicts what experiments later find.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability and statistics, with derivations checked step by step.

**Intuition half:** what each object means as a picture (a gradient as slope, an eigenvector as a stable direction, a distribution as a cloud of outcomes).

## What you can change
Level of rigour, geometric versus algebraic route, which tools are drilled.

## How it is learned
Modes M3 M5 M6. Work graded problem sets with computer algebra checking, and explain each result in a sentence and a sketch.

## Bridge test
Problems solved correctly and each answer explained in a picture that predicts a variant.
