# U-06 Picture-to-equation translation

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
A result that cannot be pictured cannot be extended, and a picture that cannot be written as an equation cannot be tested.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** formal derivation.

**Intuition half:** a diagram, a special case and a one-line reason. After each derivation, produce all three and predict a variant. Explaining examples to oneself improves understanding.

## What you can change
Choice of picture, special cases, size of the variant.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. For each topic write 'why this is true in one picture' and test a variant.

## Bridge test
The variant prediction is correct and the explanation survives expert review.
