# U-28 Language and linguistics

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung E · Mind and society

## Why it exists
Language is the medium of nearly all human knowledge.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics, information theory and statistical models of language.

**Intuition half:** feeling for register, implication and style. Meaning is not the same as form.

## What you can change
Level of analysis, language family, corpus.

## How it is learned
Modes M1 M5 M6. Analyse texts across languages with typological data and corpora.

## Bridge test
Correct analysis of structure and implied meaning across several languages.
