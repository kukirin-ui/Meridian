# R-01 Market and customer discovery

**Evidence C: emerging** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 1 · Find

## Why it matters
Most ventures fail by building what nobody pays for. Demand has to be found before work is built.

## How it works
**Logic half:** define a narrow customer and the job they need done, then count evidence of paid demand (existing spending, workarounds, repeated requests).

**Imagination half:** notice unmet needs and listen for the problem behind the stated request. Speak with 10 to 20 real prospects or read public demand signals before building anything.

## What you can change
Narrowness of the segment, number of conversations, what counts as evidence (money or commitment, not compliments).

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Run discovery on three niches and score the evidence in a log.

## Proof it works
A niche where several prospects describe the problem unprompted and at least one will pay or commit.

**Guardrail:** be honest about who you are and how data is used.
