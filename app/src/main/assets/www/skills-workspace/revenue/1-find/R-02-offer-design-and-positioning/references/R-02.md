# R-02 Offer design and positioning

**Evidence C: emerging** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 1 · Find

## Why it matters
People pay for a clear result, not for skills. A sharp offer is easier to sell and to deliver.

## How it works
State who it is for, the result, what is included and excluded, the price, the proof, and the alternative the customer would otherwise use. Package a repeatable service with a fixed scope so delivery can be checked.

## What you can change
Scope size, packaging, guarantee, proof type.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Write ten offers and test whether strangers can restate them correctly in one sentence.

## Proof it works
Prospects restate the offer accurately and can tell why it differs from alternatives.

**Guardrail:** no claim the work cannot deliver.
