# R-04 Opportunity screening and kill criteria

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 1 · Find

## Why it matters
Time is the scarce resource. Small bets with predefined limits beat open-ended projects, and scams are common.

## How it works
Estimate probability, payoff and cost for each opportunity, run the cheapest test first, and set stop rules in advance. Treat guaranteed returns, upfront fees, secrecy and pressure as warning signs.

## What you can change
Loss limit, test length, stop rule.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Keep an opportunity log with predicted and actual results.

## Proof it works
Bets are stopped at their predefined limits and warning signs are flagged before any payment.

**Guardrail:** never pay to access 'guaranteed' income.
