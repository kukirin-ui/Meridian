# R-15 Risk, fraud and ethics

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 5 · Grow and protect

## Why it matters
Short-term gains from deception destroy trust and invite penalties.

## How it works
Identify risks: payment fraud, chargebacks, scams, privacy breaches, misleading design. Refuse deceptive tactics, protect customer data, and review decisions against harms to customers and third parties.

## What you can change
Risk register, thresholds for refusal, review frequency.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Review case studies of deceptive practices and write the honest alternative.

## Proof it works
No deceptive design or claim passes review.

**Guardrail:** refuse any task that requires deception or harm.
