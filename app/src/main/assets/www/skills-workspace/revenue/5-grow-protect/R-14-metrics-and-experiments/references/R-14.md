# R-14 Metrics and experiments

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 5 · Grow and protect

## Why it matters
Improvement needs measurement that is not fooled by noise.

## How it works
Track the funnel (visits, enquiries, sales, delivery, repeat), cost to acquire, margin and retention. Test one change at a time with pre-stated success criteria and enough samples, and report null results.

## What you can change
Metric set, sample size, test length.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Run small experiments on offers and messages and record pre-stated criteria.

## Proof it works
Decisions follow results that exceed noise.

**Guardrail:** no cherry-picking or fake testimonials.
