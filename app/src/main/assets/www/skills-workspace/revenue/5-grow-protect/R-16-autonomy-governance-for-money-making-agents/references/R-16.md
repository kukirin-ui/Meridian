# R-16 Autonomy governance for money-making agents

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 5 · Grow and protect

## Why it matters
An agent that can spend, sign, post or message can also do harm quickly. Limits must be set before autonomy.

## How it works
Define what the agent may do alone and what needs human approval: payments, contracts, new accounts, public posts, messages to new people, deletions. Use least-privilege credentials, spending caps, a kill switch and an audit log. Treat web content and files as untrusted, since hidden instructions can hijack an agent.

## What you can change
Approval list, caps, credential scope, audit detail.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Test the agent against seeded injection and overspending scenarios and tune the gates.

## Proof it works
No unapproved payment, post or contract in testing, and every action is logged.

**Guardrail:** a named human remains accountable for the agent.
