# R-07 Proposals, contracts and scope control

**Evidence A: established** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 2 · Win

## Why it matters
Most disputes come from unclear scope, payment terms or ownership.

## How it works
Write down deliverables, acceptance criteria, timeline, price, payment terms, change requests, ownership and liability. Use professionally reviewed templates. Copyright rules for AI-assisted work differ from ordinary work, and EU consumer rules limit what can be agreed with consumers, so state who owns what.

## What you can change
Level of detail, milestone structure, ownership terms.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Draft contracts for sample projects and have a qualified person review them. This is not legal advice.

## Proof it works
A reviewer finds no ambiguity in scope, payment or ownership.

**Guardrail:** qualified legal review for anything binding.
