# R-11 Productised and automated service design

**Evidence C: emerging** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 3 · Deliver

## Why it matters
Automation raises income per hour only if failures are controlled.

## How it works
Split the service into steps, automate the repeatable ones, keep human approval where mistakes are costly, monitor outputs, and plan for failure. Prefer simple loops over complex autonomy.

## What you can change
Which steps are automated, approval points, monitoring.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Automate one step at a time and compare error rate and cost with the manual version.

## Proof it works
Error rate and time per job improve with no loss of quality.

**Guardrail:** human approval for money, contracts and public statements.
