# R-13 Legal, platform terms and compliance

**Evidence A: established** · Revenue Layer (shared by all agents) · Stage 4 · Get paid

## Why it matters
Legal and policy mistakes can end a business or an account.

## How it works
Know the basics of contracts, intellectual property, privacy (GDPR), advertising disclosure, consumer protection and the EU rules on AI and online services; read the terms of cloud services, APIs and marketplaces, which may limit automation, scraping, resale and account creation; disclose AI use where required. Rules vary by country and change.

## What you can change
Jurisdiction, data handled, which terms apply.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Create a compliance checklist for each service and review it against current official texts.

## Proof it works
Each service has a dated checklist of applicable rules and terms.

**Guardrail:** this is education, not legal advice.
