# L-36 Large-scale design and architecture

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 1 · Code Master (logic) · Tier 4 · Mastery

## Why it exists
Systems fail from poor boundaries more often than from poor code.

## How it is made
Split by what is likely to change, define stable interfaces, record decisions and trade-offs, plan for failure and evolution, and keep the design small enough to understand.

## What you can change
Module boundaries, coupling, which decisions are reversible.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Write design documents for three systems and have them reviewed against failure scenarios.

## Proof of mastery
The design survives a review of failure and change scenarios without rework of its core.
