# G-28 3D modelling, sculpting and texturing

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
3D assets feed games, film, product visuals and AR.

## How it works
Topology and polygon budget, subdivision, sculpting, UV unwrapping, texture painting, and material setup; export to open formats.

## Ways to vary it
Polygon budget, topology style, texture resolution, format.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Model, unwrap and texture a prop, then export and view in an engine.

## Signs of mastery
The model deforms and renders correctly with clean topology and UVs.

**Failure mode:** heavy meshes with bad topology.
