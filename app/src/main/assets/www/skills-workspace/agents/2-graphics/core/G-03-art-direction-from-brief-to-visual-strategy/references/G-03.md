# G-03 Art direction: from brief to visual strategy

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Imagination core · ideas, taste, direction

## Why it matters
Beautiful work that misses the brief fails. A visual strategy aligns audience, message, style and constraints.

## How it works
Clarify the audience, goal, message, tone, deliverables, formats and constraints; define the visual idea in one sentence; choose palette, type, imagery and layout rules; check each piece against that sentence. The double-diamond pattern alternates widening and narrowing.

## Ways to vary it
How tight the brief is, number of directions shown, who approves what.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Write strategies for ten briefs, then test whether others can reproduce the intended look from the strategy alone.

## Signs of mastery
Reviewers can predict the style from the strategy, and delivered pieces match the brief.

**Failure mode:** strategy words with no visual consequence.
