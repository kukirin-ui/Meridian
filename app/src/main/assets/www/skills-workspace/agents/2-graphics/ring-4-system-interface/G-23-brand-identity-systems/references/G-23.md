# G-23 Brand identity systems

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 4 · System and interface

## Why it matters
A brand is recognised by consistent signals, not a single logo.

## How it works
Positioning, name, mark, colour, type, imagery, voice, applications and guidelines; test recognition and consistency across touchpoints.

## Ways to vary it
Brand personality, flexibility of the system, number of touchpoints.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Develop identity systems for several fictional clients and test them in mock-ups.

## Signs of mastery
Applications look like one brand without being identical.

**Failure mode:** style with no strategy.
