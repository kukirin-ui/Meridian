# G-26 Motion graphics and animation

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
Motion explains change, directs attention and brings life to graphics.

## How it works
Timing and spacing, easing, anticipation, follow-through, staging, keyframes and graph editors, and loops. Provide reduced-motion alternatives for web.

## Ways to vary it
Duration, easing, frame rate, motion intensity.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Animate a bouncing ball, a logo and an interface transition; compare with classic principles.

## Signs of mastery
Motion looks natural, serves meaning and respects reduced-motion settings.

**Failure mode:** decoration that distracts.
