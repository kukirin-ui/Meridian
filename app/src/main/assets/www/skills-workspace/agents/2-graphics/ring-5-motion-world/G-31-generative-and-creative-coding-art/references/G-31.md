# G-31 Generative and creative-coding art

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 5 · Motion and world

## Why it matters
Code makes unlimited, controllable variations and original tools.

## How it works
Rules, randomness and noise, particles, flow fields, shaders and procedural generation; seed control so any output can be reproduced and curated.

## Ways to vary it
Rule set, seed, parameter ranges, selection method.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Create generative series with reproducible seeds and curate the best.

## Signs of mastery
A generator produces varied, controllable and reproducible outputs.

**Failure mode:** random noise mistaken for novelty.
