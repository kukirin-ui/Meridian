# G-14 Pattern, texture and ornament

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 2 · Mark and type

## Why it matters
Repeated structure creates rhythm, surface and identity.

## How it works
Tiling and seamless repeats, symmetry groups, noise and procedural texture, hand-made texture capture, and controlling density so patterns support rather than fight the content.

## Ways to vary it
Repeat type, symmetry, scale, randomness.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build seamless patterns by hand and by code and test them tiled.

## Signs of mastery
Patterns tile without visible seams and remain calm beneath text.

**Failure mode:** patterns that overwhelm content.
