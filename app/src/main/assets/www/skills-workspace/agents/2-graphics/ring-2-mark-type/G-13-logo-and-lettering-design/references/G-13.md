# G-13 Logo and lettering design

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 2 · Mark and type

## Why it matters
A mark is the compact face of an identity and must work at every size and place.

## How it works
Research and concept, sketch many routes, reduce to the simplest form, test in one colour, small, large and reversed, then build as vector with spacing rules.

## Ways to vary it
Wordmark versus symbol, level of detail, colour use.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Create concepts for several fictional brands, test them at ten sizes and backgrounds.

## Signs of mastery
The mark works in one colour at small size and is distinct from existing marks.

**Failure mode:** accidental similarity to existing marks.
