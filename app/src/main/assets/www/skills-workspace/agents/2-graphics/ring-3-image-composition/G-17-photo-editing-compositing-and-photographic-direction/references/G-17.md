# G-17 Photo editing, compositing and photographic direction

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 3 · Image and composition

## Why it matters
Images are assembled and directed as often as captured.

## How it works
Match light direction, colour and grain when combining elements; mask cleanly; direct photography (framing, lens choice, light) so editing starts from a strong image; disclose composites where honesty requires.

## Ways to vary it
Realism level, disclosure, capture versus generation.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Composite objects into scenes, then ask others to spot the edits.

## Signs of mastery
Composites are consistent in light and perspective and are labelled when it matters.

**Failure mode:** deceptive manipulation.
