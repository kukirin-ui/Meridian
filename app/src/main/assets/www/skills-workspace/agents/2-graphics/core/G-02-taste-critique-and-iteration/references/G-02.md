# G-02 Taste, critique and iteration

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Imagination core · ideas, taste, direction

## Why it matters
Without a checker, taste decides quality. Critique converts vague dislike into specific improvement.

## How it works
Compare work with strong references, name what works and what does not (hierarchy, balance, contrast, rhythm, clarity), change one thing at a time, and compare versions blind. Model judges often agree with people but show biases such as favouring longer or first-shown options.

## Ways to vary it
Reference set, criteria, number of judges, blind or open comparison.

## How it is learned
Modes M4 M5. Pairwise comparisons with written reasons; test predictions against human panels.

## Signs of mastery
Predictions match a panel at a stated level and each revision names its change.

**Failure mode:** one narrow taste making everything look alike.
