# G-19 Concept art and storyboarding

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 3 · Image and composition

## Why it matters
Planning visually saves cost in animation, games and film.

## How it works
Rapid thumbnails, silhouette tests, key frames, shot types, continuity, camera and staging, and an animatic to test pacing.

## Ways to vary it
Number of thumbnails, fidelity, frame rate of the animatic.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Storyboard a one-minute story and test it with viewers.

## Signs of mastery
Viewers understand the story from the boards alone.

**Failure mode:** pretty frames with no clear sequence.
