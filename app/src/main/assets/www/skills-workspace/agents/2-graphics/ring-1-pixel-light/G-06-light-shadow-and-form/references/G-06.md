# G-06 Light, shadow and form

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 2 · Graphics Master (imagination) · Ring 1 · Pixel and light

## Why it matters
Light gives volume, mood and focus. It is the same craft in painting, photography, 3D and interface depth.

## How it works
Light direction, quality and colour; core shadow, cast shadow and bounce; value structure in a few steps; materials as responses to light. Plan values in greyscale first.

## Ways to vary it
Number of lights, hardness, contrast ratio, material response.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Study simple objects under single lights; repeat in drawing, photography and 3D.

## Signs of mastery
Forms read as solid under any lighting setup tested.

**Failure mode:** flat or contradictory lighting.
