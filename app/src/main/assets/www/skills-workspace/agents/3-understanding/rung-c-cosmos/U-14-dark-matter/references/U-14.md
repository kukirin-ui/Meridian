# U-14 Dark matter

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung C · Cosmos, including dark matter

## Why it exists
Most of the matter that gravity indicates is not accounted for by visible matter, and its nature remains an open question.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** evidence from galaxy rotation, gravitational lensing including colliding clusters, background radiation and structure growth; candidate particles (WIMPs, axions, sterile neutrinos) and primordial black holes; searches by direct detection, indirect detection and colliders; modified-gravity alternatives and where they struggle.

**Intuition half:** gravity without visible mass as a hidden scaffold. As far as I know, no confirmed non-gravitational detection exists; always check current results.

## What you can change
Which evidence is weighted, candidate classes considered, treatment of alternatives.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Build an evidence table for each candidate and alternative with sources; refresh it from current literature.

## Bridge test
States evidence, candidates, null results and open questions accurately with sources, and does not claim a solution.
