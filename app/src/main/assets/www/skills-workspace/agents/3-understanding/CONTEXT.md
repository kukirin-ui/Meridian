# Understanding Master — routing (`agents/3-understanding`)

**Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination)** · Bridge · Logic + imagination · Engine: logic and imagination together

Agent 3 is the Understanding Master: logic and imagination on one helix. The seven rungs climb from the language of science through physics (including quantum), the cosmos (including dark matter), life, mind and society, to communication and learning, and finally to the control skills for open problems. Every card states its logic half and its imagination half, and ends with a bridge test showing the two agree.

Frontier topics are open: quantum interpretations, dark matter, dark energy, the Hubble tension and the origin of life are labelled as open, dated and re-checked (U-35). Mode choice between intuition and computation follows U-36, drawing on research into when intuition is reliable.

**Shape.** Helix of seven rungs. Blue strand: logic. Orange strand: imagination. Every rung joins both.

**Where to start.** U-03, U-35 and U-36 first, then the rung you need.

**Sources for the control rules:** [106], [188], [189], [190] (see `bibliography.md`).
