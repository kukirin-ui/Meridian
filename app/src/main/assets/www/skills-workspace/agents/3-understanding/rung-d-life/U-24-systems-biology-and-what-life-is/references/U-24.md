# U-24 Systems biology and what life is

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
Life is more than parts: it is networks of interactions that sustain themselves.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** network models, feedback loops, stochastic dynamics, information flow in cells.

**Intuition half:** life as organised processes rather than a list of ingredients. There is no single agreed definition of life.

## What you can change
Definition used, scale, level of simplification.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Simulate a small gene-regulation network and compare behaviours.

## Bridge test
Simulation shows the expected switches and oscillations, and the definitions of life are presented with their strengths and limits.
