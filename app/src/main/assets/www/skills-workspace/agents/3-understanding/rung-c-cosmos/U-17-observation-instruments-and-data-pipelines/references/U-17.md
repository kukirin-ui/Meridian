# U-17 Observation, instruments and data pipelines

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung C · Cosmos, including dark matter

## Why it exists
All knowledge of the cosmos comes through instruments, and their systematics shape every conclusion.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** calibration, noise models, signal extraction, error propagation, archives and software tools.

**Intuition half:** a feel for what a detector can and cannot see and how artefacts look.

## What you can change
Instrument model, calibration strategy, how systematics are tested.

## How it is learned
Modes M3 M5 M6. Process public telescope or detector data end to end and test with injected fake signals.

## Bridge test
Injected signals are recovered at the expected rate and artefacts are identified.
