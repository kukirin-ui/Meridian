# U-08 Electromagnetism, waves and optics

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
Fields and waves underlie light, radio, electronics and much of quantum theory.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** Maxwell's equations, wave solutions, boundary conditions, interference.

**Intuition half:** field lines, ripples and how a wave bends, reflects and interferes.

## What you can change
Vector or differential form, ray versus wave model.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Derive wave equations, then simulate interference patterns.

## Bridge test
Simulated patterns match derived ones and limiting cases are explained.
