# U-25 Cognition, psychology and behaviour

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung E · Mind and society

## Why it exists
To communicate and to teach, an agent must model how people actually think and where they err.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** replicated findings, experimental design, heuristics and biases, theory of mind tasks.

**Intuition half:** empathy and perspective. Weigh findings by replication.

## What you can change
Evidence required, population, theory versus data.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Read primary studies with replications and sort them.

## Bridge test
Separates replicated from unreplicated findings with sources.
