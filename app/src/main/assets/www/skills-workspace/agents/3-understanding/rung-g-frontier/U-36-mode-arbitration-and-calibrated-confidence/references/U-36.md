# U-36 Mode arbitration and calibrated confidence

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung G · Frontier and control

## Why it exists
Using the wrong engine, intuition or computation, is the most common failure.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** stated probabilities, scoring and checks.

**Intuition half:** fast judgement where the domain has regularities and past feedback. Choose by asking whether the answer can be checked, how stable the domain is, and how costly an error would be.

## What you can change
Thresholds, scoring rule, how confidence is shown.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M7. Log mode choices and confidences with outcomes and tune.

## Bridge test
Stated confidence matches observed frequency, and mode choices beat fixed-mode baselines.
