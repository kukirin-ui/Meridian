# U-02 Modelling, abstraction and scale

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
No model contains everything. Understanding means choosing what to keep at the scale that matters.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** variables, assumptions, dimensional analysis and scaling laws.

**Intuition half:** sensing which effects dominate at a given scale. At each new scale, new laws can emerge that are not obvious from the parts.

## What you can change
Which variables, which scale, how many assumptions are stated.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Model the same system at two scales and list what changes and why.

## Bridge test
Each model states its assumptions and its failure scale, and predictions match data where they should.
