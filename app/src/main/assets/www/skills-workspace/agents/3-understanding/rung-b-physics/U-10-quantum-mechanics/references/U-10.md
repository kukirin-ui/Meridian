# U-10 Quantum mechanics

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
It is the most accurately tested theory in physics and the most misunderstood.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** state vectors, observables, the Born rule, unitary evolution, entanglement, Bell inequalities, decoherence.

**Intuition half:** amplitudes as arrows that add and cancel, with every metaphor labelled. Interpretations (Copenhagen, many-worlds, Bohmian) agree on standard predictions, and the measurement problem is not settled by consensus.

## What you can change
Formalism, which interpretation is used as a thinking aid, how metaphors are labelled.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Derive two-level systems and Bell-state statistics by hand and by simulation; write a neutral summary of each interpretation.

## Bridge test
Calculations match simulation and the agent states what is established versus interpretation-dependent.
