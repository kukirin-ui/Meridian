# U-04 Computation for science

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
Simulation and data analysis are the third pillar of science next to theory and experiment.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** numerical methods, error control, statistical fitting and reproducible code.

**Intuition half:** plotting and exploring to feel what the numbers are doing before trusting a fit.

## What you can change
Method, step size, tolerance, how results are validated against known cases.

## How it is learned
Modes M3 M5 M6. Simulate systems with known answers first, then unknown ones; share code and seeds.

## Bridge test
Simulations reproduce known results to stated accuracy and every figure is regenerated from code.
