# U-26 Social systems, economics and institutions

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung E · Mind and society

## Why it exists
Groups behave differently from individuals, and incentives shape outcomes.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** networks, game theory, models of segregation, commons problems and information asymmetry.

**Intuition half:** seeing how local rules produce large patterns. Test models against data.

## What you can change
Network structure, incentive design, population size.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Re-implement classic models and compare with real cases.

## Bridge test
Reproduces classic results and explains which assumption drives them.
