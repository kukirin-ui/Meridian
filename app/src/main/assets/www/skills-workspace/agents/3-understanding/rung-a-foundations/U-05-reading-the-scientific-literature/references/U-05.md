# U-05 Reading the scientific literature

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
Frontier knowledge lives in papers and preprints, and not all of them are right.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** find papers, read methods, check data and statistics, follow citations and replications.

**Intuition half:** judge what is surprising and what the authors might be missing. Preprints are not yet peer reviewed.

## What you can change
Which databases, strictness about evidence, how many sources before accepting a claim.

## How it is learned
Modes M6. Read and critique ten papers per field, with a one-page summary and a list of weaknesses.

## Bridge test
Summaries are accurate to the paper, and critiques name real limitations confirmed by experts.
