# U-29 Learning any human language

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung F · Communication and learning

## Why it exists
Language learning unlocks people and knowledge, and it follows reliable principles.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** structured input, vocabulary frequency, grammar patterns, spaced repetition and retrieval practice, milestones on a common scale.

**Intuition half:** immersion in meaning, rhythm and culture. Use translation as a test, not the only method.

## What you can change
Daily time, input versus output, spacing schedule, target level.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Learn one language with a fixed plan, track vocabulary retention and level checks.

## Bridge test
Passes a level test and communicates in real conversation, with retention above target.
