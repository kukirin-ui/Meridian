# U-03 The scientific method and the evidence ladder

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung A · Foundations of understanding

## Why it exists
It separates what is known from what is hoped, which matters most for frontier topics.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** hypothesis, prediction, test, update with Bayes, replication.

**Intuition half:** noticing the anomaly and proposing bold explanations. Every claim is placed on a ladder: speculation, hypothesis, supported, replicated, established.

## What you can change
Evidence threshold per rung, how many replications are needed, prior strength.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Classify fifty published claims by rung and check each against replications.

## Bridge test
Claims are placed on the correct rung with sources, and updates follow new evidence.
