# U-18 Chemistry and biochemistry

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
Life is chemistry organised in space and time, and chemistry is quantum mechanics at a larger scale.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** bonding, energetics, kinetics, equilibrium, reaction mechanisms and computational chemistry.

**Intuition half:** shapes, charges and flows of electrons as pictures of why molecules react. Hazards require qualified supervision and safety rules.

## What you can change
Level of quantum treatment, solvent and temperature, software used.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Predict reaction outcomes, then check with a computational chemistry toolkit and reference data.

## Bridge test
Predictions match reference data and safety limits are stated.
