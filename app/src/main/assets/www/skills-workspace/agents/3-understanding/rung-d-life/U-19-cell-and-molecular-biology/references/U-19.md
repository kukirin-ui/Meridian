# U-19 Cell and molecular biology

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung D · Life

## Why it exists
The cell is the unit of life, and DNA, RNA and proteins are its working parts.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** structure of DNA, the flow from DNA to RNA to protein, membranes, signalling, protein structure and function.

**Intuition half:** the cell as a crowded, moving factory. Structure prediction now rivals experiments for many proteins.

## What you can change
Organism, scale, level of molecular detail.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Trace a gene to its protein and view structures in the open structure database.

## Bridge test
Explains a pathway from gene to function with correct molecular steps.
