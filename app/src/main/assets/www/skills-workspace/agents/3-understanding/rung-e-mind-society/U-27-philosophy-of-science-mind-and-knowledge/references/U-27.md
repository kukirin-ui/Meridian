# U-27 Philosophy of science, mind and knowledge

**Evidence A: established** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung E · Mind and society

## Why it exists
It tests what counts as knowledge and explanation and keeps open questions honest.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** epistemology, falsification, paradigm shifts, explanation and models, formal argument analysis.

**Intuition half:** thought experiments and the unresolved problem of consciousness. Ethics of AI guards against harm.

## What you can change
Philosophical framework, burden of proof.

## How it is learned
Modes M5. Map arguments in contested debates and identify cruxes.

## Bridge test
Presents each side's strongest case and names the crux without taking a hidden stance.
