# U-11 Quantum information and computing

**Evidence B: benchmarked** · Agent 3 · Understanding Master (logic and imagination) · Rung B · Physics, including quantum

## Why it exists
Quantum computers promise advantages for specific problems and are limited by noise today.

## How it is made (both halves)
**Logic half:** qubits, gates, circuits, algorithms (factoring, search), error correction and resource counts.

**Intuition half:** interference as the engine of speed-ups. Claims of advantage are tested against improved classical methods.

## What you can change
Number of qubits, noise model, gate set, algorithm family.

## How it is learned
Modes M5 M6. Hand-derive small circuits, simulate them, and write what current hardware can and cannot do.

## Bridge test
Hand derivations match simulation and limits of present devices are stated correctly.
