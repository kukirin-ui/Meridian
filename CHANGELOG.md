# Releases

Current version: **2.2.0** (`versionCode` 39).

Change the version only in `app/build.gradle.kts`. About in the app reads that number from the installed APK. The debug APK artifact is named `Meridian-<versionName>-debug`.

`original/` is the archived **1.8.6** source. New work goes in `app/`.

## 2.2.0

The paid version is everything. One Revolut link. The payer chooses any whole euro from €1 to €1,000,000, and both ends unlock the same app. The amount is saved on the phone and copied before checkout opens.

## 2.1.0

OLED look, settings, shortcuts, hardware-report share, and the sellable surface on top of 2.0.

## 2.0.0

Gradle app, offline licence keys, and a 7-day trial. 1.8.6 was moved to `original/`.

## 1.8.6
